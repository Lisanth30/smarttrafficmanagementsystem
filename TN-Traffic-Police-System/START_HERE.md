## 🚦 Traffic Signal Guide

### What You'll See:
```
            NORTH
              ↓↑
    WEST ← ← ⊕ → → EAST
              ↓↑
            SOUTH
```

- **Rectangles** 🟦 = Cars
- **Lines** ━ = Bikes
- **Green** 🟢 = GO
- **Yellow** 🟡 = SLOW
- **Red** 🔴 = STOP

### Controls:
- **Start** = Begin traffic
- **Pause** = Stop temporarily  
- **Reset** = Clear everything
